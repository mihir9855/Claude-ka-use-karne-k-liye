# Design: Stickers (neo-brutalist)

Flat bright tiles on a soft page colour, thick black outlines, hard offset shadows with no blur, chunky grotesque type.
Mode: Operate (task first, brand in details).

## Rules
- **Tile:** 2.5 px outline in ink (cream in dark mode), a 4 px hard shadow pushed down and right, radius 18. Pressing a tile slides it into its shadow.
- **Colour:** by day the page is Paper (#F3EFE6) with black outlines and bright tiles. By night it is a blackboard (#161616) with chalk-white writing and outlines, chalk-pastel tiles, and shadows like chalk dust. Text on tiles is always near-black.
- **Type:** Oooh Baby (handwritten script) for titles, headings and dish names, Archivo Black for numbers, Space Grotesk for small text.
- **Alignment:** everything sits on one left edge (20 px). The right padding is 16 px so a tile's shadow ends where the left gutter would be. Tiles in a row are equal width and height. No tilted tiles.
- **Text:** one line per tile, with an ellipsis.
- **Navigation:** five bottom destinations; the active one is a yellow outlined pill. Nutrition opens from Home with a back arrow.
- **Research basis:** neobrutalism uses 2 to 5 px borders, zero-blur offset shadows, flat saturated fills, and black text on bright accents.
