# Design: Haldi

Loud turmeric colour blocks, chunky grotesque type (Bricolage Grotesque), ivory or near-black pages, one chilli accent. Mode: Operate (task first, brand in details).

## World
- **Paper (light):** page #F6F0E4, card #FFFBF3, ink #2B2118, muted #75675A (4.5:1 on paper), hairline #E3D8C3, accent terracotta #B5472B with #FFF8EE on it, olive #4F6B3A for done, brick #A32D2D for danger.
- **Night kitchen (dark):** page #1C1713, card #26201A, ink #F2E9DA, muted #A8998A, hairline #382F26, accent #E0805F with #1C1713 on it.
- **Type:** Fraunces (serif) for headings and numerals, Manrope for everything else. Weights are real font files.
- **Shape:** 14 px cards, hairline borders, no shadows. Lists are ruled with hairlines instead of nested cards.
- **Signature:** the day is a "page": a serif date heading, then a ruled table of meals with times in the left margin.
- **Navigation:** five bottom destinations (Home, Chat, Menu, Lists, Settings). Nutrition opens from Home with a back arrow.
