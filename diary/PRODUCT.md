# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

android

## Users
Indian families (up to six people on one phone) who want to remember what they eat without filling forms. Used many times a day for a few seconds: type a meal, glance at what comes next, tick a grocery item. Mostly Hinglish speakers; the UI is English only.

## Product Purpose
Khana Diary is a food diary that learns. You only type names of what you ate; it remembers habits, shows today's menu and what is coming up by the clock, plans a week, tracks nutrition against a personal goal, and keeps grocery and other lists filled from the same chat box.

## Positioning
No forms and no logging screens: one chat line is the whole input, and the menu is built from the family's own habits.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Chat logging with meal, count and typo detection; Home with today's menu and Upcoming; week Menu; Nutrition; Lists (grocery and others, filled from chat); Settings (profiles, theme, backup, export, version). Free, offline, no accounts. Single-line tile text with ellipsis. Dark and light themes. No APK until the owner says so.

## Brand Commitments
Name: Khana Diary. English UI only.

## Product Principles
1. Typing a meal should take seconds and never feel like a form.
2. Show what to eat next before showing numbers.
3. Everything on a tile fits on one line.
4. The app must work with an empty history and get better with use.
