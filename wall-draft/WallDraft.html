Project

Default sizes

Every new wall, room, door and window starts with these sizes. Thickness and height you type when adding a room are kept here too. They are remembered on this phone, also for new projects.

Drawing walls

✎ Hand: draw walls with your finger; they are straightened for you. ╱ Line: tap each corner, or drag one straight wall. ▭ Box: drag corner to corner for four walls. Walls that touch or cross are joined into one, and any closed area becomes a room with its size and area. While drawing a line, type a length and tap an arrow to place the next corner exactly.

Walls of this room · tap one to edit it or add a door or window

Drag the selected room on the plan to move it. It comes away from rooms next to it, and sticks to them again when you drop it touching.

Measurements on plan

Selected measurement

Selected reference line

Walls, corners and new walls snap onto reference lines and where they cross. Reference lines are not printed.

Reference lines: select a wall and tap “Reference line…” to place one at an exact distance (e.g. 35.5"), or use ⋯ Ref line. Drag a wall onto it and it snaps there.

Tap ↔ Measure, then drag between two points, or tap them one after the other. It snaps to corners, inside corners, wall faces, door and window edges and columns. Drag a measurement to move its line.

Kitchen

Selected platform

Drag it on the plan: a platform slides along walls with its back to the wall; a sink or hob sits on a platform.

Kitchen setup builds the platform along the walls of a room (straight, L, U or parallel) and places the sink and hob. Or pick ▬ Platform and drag along a wall, then tap ◫ Sink or ⊚ Hob onto it.

Columns & beams

Selected column

▣ Column: tap to place; it snaps to wall corners and along walls. ┅ Beam: drag, or tap two points. Wall elevations show the columns in each wall and the beams above it.
