### 8. Risk Assessment Framework

#### **Portfolio Impact Calculation**
```
SECTOR EXPOSURE = (Sector Weight × Sensitivity Factor)
PORTFOLIO RISK = Σ(Sector Exposure × Policy Impact)
```

#### **Sensitivity Factors**
- **Critical**: 1.0 (Direct revenue impact >30%)
- **High**: 0.7 (Revenue impact 15-30%)
- **Medium**: 0.4 (Revenue impact 5-15%)
- **Low**: 0.1 (Revenue impact <5%)

#### **Risk Mitigation Strategies**
```
PRE-BUDGET:
- Reduce critical/high exposure positions
- Increase cash allocation (20-30%)
- Hedge with options strategies

BUDGET WEEK:
- Maintain minimal exposure (30-40% of normal)
- Focus on defensive sectors
- Monitor news flow closely

POST-BUDGET:
- Rebalance based on actual announcements
- Scale into opportunities gradually
- Maintain wider stop losses
```

```python
def calculate_sector_exposure(portfolio, policy_change):
    """
    Calculate portfolio exposure to specific policy changes
    """
    total_exposure = 0
    for stock, weight in portfolio.items():
        sector = get_stock_sector(stock)
        sensitivity = get_sector_sensitivity(sector, policy_change)
        exposure = weight * sensitivity
        total_exposure += exposure
    return total_exposure
```
