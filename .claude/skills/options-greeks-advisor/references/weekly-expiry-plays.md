# Weekly Expiry Plays for Indian Options

## Weekly Expiry Characteristics
- **Expiry Day**: Every Thursday
- **Time Decay**: Accelerated theta last 2 days
- **Gamma Risk**: Highest Wednesday-Thursday
- **Liquidity**: Thinnest on Wednesday morning
- **IV Pattern**: Peaks Tuesday, drops Thursday

## Wednesday Strategies

### 1. Straddle Sell (Core Theta Play)
- **Entry Time**: 9:20 AM IST (after opening volatility)
- **Strike Selection**: ATM ±50 points
- **Max Cost**: 1.5% of straddle value
- **Target Theta**: >₹800/day
- **Exit Rules**:
  - Profit: 40% of max profit
  - Loss: 50% of premium collected
  - Time: 2:30 PM IST

### 2. Iron Condor (Low Risk Theta)
- **Entry Time**: Wednesday 10:00 AM
- **Wing Width**: 200-300 points
- **Credit Target**: ₹300-500
- **Max Loss**: 2x credit received
- **Delta Target**: -0.05 to +0.05
- **Exit**: Thursday 1:00 PM or 30% credit decay

### 3. OTM Call/Put Sell
- **Entry Time**: Wednesday afternoon
- **Strike Selection**: 200-300 points OTM
- **Premium Target**: ₹50-100 per lot
- **Delta Max**: 0.15
- **Exit**: 50% premium decay or Thursday close

## Thursday Strategies

### 4. Morning OTM Scalp
- **Entry Time**: 9:15-9:45 AM IST
- **Strike Selection**: 100-200 points OTM
- **Hold Time**: 15-30 minutes
- **Target**: 2:1 reward-risk
- **Volume Confirm**: >1.5x average

### 5. Expiry Day Iron Fly
- **Entry Time**: Thursday 9:30 AM
- **Structure**: Sell ATM, buy OTM calls/puts
- **Credit Target**: ₹200-400
- **Max Loss**: 1.5x credit
- **Exit**: 2:30 PM or target hit

### 6. Directional Debit Spread
- **Entry Time**: Thursday morning
- **Width**: 100-200 points
- **Max Cost**: ₹300-500
- **Target**: 1:1.5 reward-risk
- **Bias**: Follow Supertrend or VWAP

## Friday-Monday Setup

### 7. Pre-Weekly Strangle
- **Entry Time**: Friday afternoon
- **Strike Selection**: 200-400 points OTM
- **Hold**: Through weekend
- **Rationale**: Lower IV, position for Monday gap

### 8. Monday Morning Adjustment
- **Review Time**: 9:15 AM Monday
- **Action**: Close/reduce if gap >1%
- **Add**: If gap small and direction confirmed

## Risk Management

### Position Sizing
- **Max Per Strategy**: 2% portfolio
- **Max Weekly Options**: 5% portfolio
- **Portfolio Theta**: Target +₹2,000/day
- **Portfolio Vega**: Keep <0.5

### Exit Rules
- **Time Stop**: 2:30 PM Thursday
- **Profit Target**: 30-50% of premium
- **Loss Limit**: 1x premium collected
- **Gamma Alert**: Close if gamma >0.08

### Adjustment Rules
- **Delta Hedge**: If position delta >±0.1
- **Roll Out**: If theta decays 40%
- **Width Reduction**: If credit doubles

## Market Condition Filters

### Favorable Conditions
- **IV Percentile**: 40-70%
- **VIX**: <20
- **Range**: Previous day range <1.5%
- **Volume**: Normal to above normal

### Unfavorable Conditions
- **IV Percentile**: >80%
- **VIX**: >25
- **News Events**: RBI, earnings, budget
- **Wide Ranges**: Previous day >2%

## Common Mistakes
1. **Early Entry**: Wednesday morning illiquidity
2. **Holding Too Long**: Thursday afternoon gamma risk
3. **Ignoring IV**: Event-driven spikes
4. **Poor Sizing**: Overexposure to single strategy
5. **Weekend Risk**: Gap risk on Monday
