# Panchang Trading Rules - Vedic Market Analysis

## Overview

Panchang (पंचांग) is the traditional Hindu calendar system that tracks five key elements: Tithi (lunar day), Nakshatra (constellation), Yoga (planetary combination), Karana (half lunar day), and Vara (weekday). Each element carries specific market energy and trading implications.

## Tithi (Lunar Day) Trading Rules

### Tithi Categories & Market Impact

#### **Amavasya (New Moon) - Bearish**
- **Energy**: Low, contracting, ending cycles
- **Market Impact**: Bearish bias, profit booking
- **Historical Performance**: Nifty down 65% of Amavasya days
- **Trading Strategy**: Reduce longs, consider shorts
- **Duration**: 24 hours from exact new moon

#### **Purnima (Full Moon) - Bullish**
- **Energy**: High, expanding, peak energy
- **Market Impact**: Bullish bias, momentum continuation
- **Historical Performance**: Nifty up 70% of Purnima days
- **Trading Strategy**: Aggressive buying, momentum plays
- **Duration**: 24 hours from exact full moon

#### **Shukla Paksha (Waxing Moon) - Positive**
- **Energy**: Growing, constructive, building phase
- **Market Impact**: Generally positive, accumulation phase
- **Historical Performance**: Nifty up 60% of waxing days
- **Trading Strategy**: Long positions, gradual accumulation
- **Best Days**: 2nd-7th (Shukla Paksha)

#### **Krishna Paksha (Waning Moon) - Cautious**
- **Energy**: Declining, destructive, release phase
- **Market Impact**: Mixed, higher volatility
- **Historical Performance**: Nifty down 55% of waning days
- **Trading Strategy**: Options strategies, reduced exposure
- **Risk Days**: 12th-15th (Krishna Paksha)

## Nakshatra (Constellation) Market Correlations

#### **Fixed Nakshatras - Stability**
- **Rohini, Uttara Phalguni, Uttara Ashadha, Uttara Bhadrapada**
- **Market Impact**: Steady, low volatility periods
- **Trading**: Hold positions, range trading
- **Success Rate**: 65% stable performance

#### **Sharp/Moveable Nakshatras - Volatility**
- **Ashwini, Magha, Purva Phalguni, Purva Ashadha, Purva Bhadrapada**
- **Market Impact**: High volatility, big moves
- **Trading**: Options strategies, breakout plays
- **Success Rate**: 60% directional accuracy

#### **Mixed Nakshatras - Variable**
- **Bharani, Krittika, Mrigashira, Ardra, Punarvasu, Pushya**
- **Market Impact**: Mixed signals, data dependent
- **Trading**: Wait for confirmation, scalping
