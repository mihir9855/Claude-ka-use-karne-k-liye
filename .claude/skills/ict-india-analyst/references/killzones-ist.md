# Killzones in IST for Indian Markets

## Opening Killzone (9:15 AM - 10:30 AM IST)

### Characteristics
- **Displacement**: Highest price movement of the day
- **Volume**: Peak volume in first 30-45 minutes
- **Participants**: FIIs, prop firms most active
- **Direction**: Often continues overnight gap direction

### What to Look For
- **Order Blocks**: Institutional positioning after open
- **FVG Formation**: Initial gaps from pre-open auction
- **Liquidity Sweeps**: Breaking of PDH/PDL levels
- **SMT Divergence**: Early signs of institutional flow

### Best Setups
- **Bullish**: Break above opening range with volume
- **Bearish**: Rejection at PDH with order block
- **FVG Fill**: Mitigation of overnight gap
- **Order Block**: Rejection candles with volume spike

### When to Avoid
- **First 15 minutes**: Extreme opening volatility
- **No Clear Direction**: Choppy movement without bias
- **Low Volume**: Below average participation

## Midday Killzone (11:00 AM - 1:00 PM IST)

### Characteristics
- **Activity**: Lowest volume period
- **Movement**: Range-bound consolidation
- **News Flow**: Lunch hour news digestion
- **Participants**: Retail traders dominant

### What to Look For
- **Structure Shifts**: Change from bullish to bearish or vice versa
- **Range Breakouts**: False breakouts to trap retail
- **Accumulation**: Institutional building positions quietly
- **FVG Mitigation**: Filling of morning gaps

### Trading Approach
- **Avoid Trading**: Wait for PM killzone
- **Monitor Only**: Note key levels for afternoon
- **Position Adjustment**: Reduce size if holding positions
- **News Watch**: Be ready for post-lunch news

## PM Killzone (1:30 PM - 3:30 PM IST)

### Characteristics
- **Energy**: Second wind after lunch
- **Volume**: Increases as close approaches
- **Direction**: Often continuation of morning trend
- **Time Pressure**: Last 2 hours create urgency

### What to Look For
- **Order Block Mitigation**: Breaking of morning order blocks
- **Liquidity Sweeps**: Final institutional positioning
- **FVG Tests**: Re-testing of unfilled gaps
- **SMT Confirmation**: Divergence resolution

### Best Setups
- **Continuation**: Following morning trend with volume
- **Reversal**: Breaking morning structure with conviction
- **Scalp Trades**: Quick 15-30 minute moves
- **End-of-Day Momentum**: Last hour imbalance

### When to Avoid
- **Last 30 minutes**: Closing auction volatility
- **Against Trend**: Strong counter-trend moves
- **Low Volume**: Below average afternoon participation

## Last Hour Focus (2:30 PM - 3:30 PM IST)

### Characteristics
- **Imbalance**: Closing price discovery
- **Volume**: Final volume spike
- **Participants**: All market participants active
- **Risk**: Highest volatility near close

### What to Look For
- **Closing Imbalance**: Large orders affecting close
- **Gamma Effects**: Options expiry impact
- **Stop Running**: Institutions triggering stops
- **Final Sweeps**: Last liquidity taking

### Trading Approach
- **Very Short Timeframes**: 1-5 minute charts
- **Tight Stops**: Quick entries and exits
- **Small Size**: Reduced position sizing
- **Exit by 3:15 PM**: Avoid closing volatility

## Killzone Entry Rules

### Opening Killzone
1. Wait for first 15 minutes to settle
2. Identify PDH/PDL levels
3. Look for order block formation
4. Enter on break/retest with volume

### PM Killzone
1. Review morning structure
2. Wait for volume increase after 1:30
3. Confirm SMT divergence if present
4. Enter on high-probability setups only

## Common Mistakes
1. **Trading Midday**: Low probability period
2. **Holding Too Long**: Missing exit signals
3. **Ignoring Volume**: Volume confirms all setups
4. **Overtrading**: Multiple setups in one killzone
5. **Last Hour Risk**: Extreme volatility near close
