# ICT Methodology Adapted for Indian Markets

## Core ICT Concepts in Indian Context

### Fair Value Gaps (FVG)
- **Definition**: Unmitigated price gaps in the order book
- **Indian Adaptation**: Overnight gaps treated as FVGs
- **Identification**: Three-candle pattern with gap between candles 1-3
- **Types**: Bullish (price gaps up), Bearish (price gaps down)
- **Mitigation**: Price returning to fill the gap

### Inverse Fair Value Gaps (IFVG)
- **Definition**: FVGs that have been mitigated but still relevant
- **Indian Context**: Previous day's gaps that influence current session
- **Relevance**: IFVGs act as support/resistance levels
- **Time Decay**: IFVGs lose relevance after 3-5 trading days

### Order Blocks
- **Definition**: Institutional order clusters
- **Indian Adaptation**: Use PDH/PDL as liquidity levels
- **Identification**: Rejection candles with volume
- **Types**: Bullish (above rejection), Bearish (below rejection)
- **Mitigation**: Price breaking and closing beyond order block

## SMT Divergence (Indian Adaptation)

### What is SMT?
- **Standard**: Smart Money Tool - divergence between indices
- **Indian Version**: Nifty vs BankNifty divergence
- **Rationale**: BankNifty more sensitive to interest rates/FII flows
- **Signal**: When Nifty and BankNifty move in opposite directions

### SMT Types
- **Bullish SMT**: Nifty up, BankNifty down (institutional buying)
- **Bearish SMT**: Nifty down, BankNifty up (institutional selling)
- **Neutral SMT**: Both moving in same direction
- **Strength**: Measured by divergence magnitude and duration

### SMT Interpretation
- **Short-term**: 5-15 minute divergences signal quick moves
- **Medium-term**: 1-2 hour divergences indicate trend changes
- **Long-term**: Daily SMT predicts next day direction
- **Confirmation**: Volume and order flow analysis

## Killzones Adapted to IST

### Opening Killzone (9:15-10:30 AM IST)
- **Purpose**: Most displacement occurs here
- **Focus**: Order block formation, initial FVG creation
- **Best Setups**: Liquidity sweeps, break of value
- **Avoid**: First 15 minutes (opening volatility)

### Midday Killzone (11:00 AM-1:00 PM IST)
- **Purpose**: Accumulation/consolidation phase
- **Focus**: Avoid trading, monitor for structure shifts
- **Activity**: Lower volume, range-bound movement
- **Setup**: Wait for PM killzone

### PM Killzone (1:30-3:30 PM IST)
- **Purpose**: Continuation or reversal moves
- **Focus**: Order block mitigation, FVG fills
- **Best Setups**: End-of-day momentum, liquidity sweeps
- **Time Pressure**: Last hour has closing imbalance

## Key Differences from US Markets

### Market Structure
- **Hours**: 6.25 hours vs 6.5 hours (shorter Indian session)
- **Liquidity**: F&O provides deep liquidity
- **Participants**: Mix of retail, DII, FII, prop firms
- **News Flow**: Event-driven (RBI, earnings, budget)

### No Overnight Session
- **Gap Impact**: Previous day close to open gaps are critical
- **Preparation**: Pre-market analysis focuses on gap as FVG
- **Overnight News**: Global events affect opening direction
- **Positioning**: Institutions position during regular hours

### FII/DII Influence
- **COT Equivalent**: FII/DII data replaces COT reports
- **Flow Analysis**: Cash vs F&O segment analysis
- **Correlation**: FII selling often leads to SMT signals
- **Timing**: FII flows strongest in opening hours

### Expiry Effects
- **Weekly Expiry**: Thursday gamma effects
- **Monthly Expiry**: Last Thursday of month
- **Options Flow**: Impacts order block formation
- **OI Changes**: Weekly OI buildup affects liquidity

## Order Flow Analysis

### Indian Market Participants
- **FIIs**: Large institutional players
- **DIIs**: Domestic institutions (LIC, mutual funds)
- **Prop Firms**: High-frequency algorithmic traders
- **Retail**: Individual traders (noise in order flow)

### Reading Order Flow
- **Volume Profile**: High volume areas as order blocks
- **Time & Sales**: Large orders indicate institutional activity
- **Bid-Ask Spread**: Tight spreads = institutional presence
- **Order Book**: Wall of bids/offers at key levels

## Risk Management
- **Position Size**: 1-2% per ICT setup
- **Stop Loss**: Beyond order block or FVG level
- **Time Exit**: End of killzone if no trigger
- **Portfolio Risk**: Max 3% daily drawdown

## Performance Expectations
- **Win Rate**: 60-70% in proper killzones
- **Avg RR**: 1:2 to 1:3 on successful setups
- **Hold Time**: 15-45 minutes per trade
- **Best Conditions**: High volume, clear SMT divergence
