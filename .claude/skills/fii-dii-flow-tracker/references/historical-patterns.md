# Historical FII Behavior Patterns — NotSoSEBI

## Global Risk-Off Events
**2020 COVID Crash:** FIIs sold ₹1.2 lakh Cr in March 2020, accelerating NIFTY decline. Recovered strongly post-June with ₹2.8 lakh Cr buying.

**2022 US Rate Hikes:** FII selling peaked at ₹45,000 Cr/month during June-August 2022. NIFTY fell 1,500 points as global liquidity tightened.

## DII Counter-Balance Role
**2020 Crisis:** DIIs bought ₹80,000 Cr while FIIs sold ₹1 lakh Cr, providing market stability.

**2022 Volatility:** DII buying increased during FII outflows, absorbing selling pressure and reducing downside.

## US Fed Decision Patterns
**Rate Hike Announcements:** FII selling 2-3 days before Fed meetings, buying resumes post-hike if "dovish."

**2023 Pivot:** FIIs bought ₹15,000 Cr in December 2023 anticipating Fed pause, driving NIFTY 2,000 point rally.

## Pre-Budget Positioning
**Union Budget Weeks:** FIIs reduce exposure 3-5 days pre-budget, resume post-announcement based on fiscal stance.

**2024 Budget:** FIIs sold ₹25,000 Cr pre-budget, bought ₹40,000 Cr post-positive reforms announcement.

## INR Depreciation Response
**USD/INR Above 83:** FII selling intensifies due to hedging costs. 2023 saw ₹60,000 Cr outflows when INR weakened to 83.50.

**Recovery Pattern:** FII buying resumes when INR stabilizes below 82, as seen in late 2023 rally.

## Sector Allocation History
**Overweight Sectors:**
- **IT:** 25-30% of FII holdings (vs 15% in NIFTY), consistent since 2010
- **Pharma:** 8-10% weighting, defensive during volatility

**Underweight Sectors:**
- **PSU Banks:** 2-3% (vs 8% in NIFTY), avoid due to governance concerns
- **Public Sector:** Minimal exposure despite market weight

**Rotation Pattern:** IT overweight during risk-on, pharma during risk-off, PSU banks avoided consistently.</content>
