# FII/DII Data Interpretation — NotSoSEBI

## Cash vs F&O Data
**Cash Market Data:** Shows actual buying/selling of shares. Reliable for current positioning but slow-moving. Represents ~70% of institutional activity.

**F&O Data:** Shows directional bets via derivatives. More volatile, faster signals. Index futures/options reveal market direction bias.

## Why F&O Matters More
F&O data predicts direction because institutions use derivatives for leveraged bets. Cash flows follow F&O signals. F&O shows conviction — large positions indicate strong views.

## Long/Short Ratio Interpretation
**Ratio = Long Positions / Short Positions**
- **Above 3.0:** Very bullish — institutions heavily long, expect upside
- **2.0-3.0:** Bullish — moderate long bias
- **1.0-2.0:** Neutral — balanced positioning
- **0.5-1.0:** Bearish — short bias emerging
- **Below 0.5:** Very bearish — heavily short, expect downside

## Combining Cash + F&O
**Full Picture Framework:**
1. **F&O Direction:** Primary signal (faster, more predictive)
2. **Cash Confirmation:** Validates F&O when aligned
3. **Divergence Alert:** Watch when cash contradicts F&O
4. **Volume Context:** High F&O volume = stronger signals

## Lag Effect Understanding
**FII Buying Today → Price Impact Over 2-3 Days**
- Day 1: F&O positioning (immediate signal)
- Day 2-3: Cash market follow-through
- Week 1: Full price impact realized
- Reason: Derivatives lead, cash follows momentum

## Sector Rotation Signals
**Holdings Data Reveals Institutional Themes:**
- **Increasing Sector Weight:** FII buying theme, expect outperformance
- **Decreasing Weight:** Profit-taking or rotation out
- **New Positions:** Fresh interest in sector
- **Heavy Concentration:** Risk if >40% in one sector
