# Earnings Season Patterns - India Earnings Calendar

## Overview

Earnings season patterns in the Indian market exhibit distinct cyclical behaviors influenced by corporate calendar, economic cycles, and market psychology. Understanding these patterns is crucial for successful earnings-based trading strategies.

## Quarterly Earnings Cycles

### Q4 Earnings Season (January-March)

#### **Season Characteristics**
- **Duration**: January 1 - March 31
- **Peak Period**: Last week of March (annual results deadline)
- **Market Volatility**: Highest of all quarters (40-60% increase)
- **Economic Context**: Union Budget impact, tax planning

#### **Key Patterns**
- **Volume Spike**: 150-200% above average daily volume
- **Index Impact**: Nifty moves 8-12% during peak weeks
- **Sector Rotation**: PSU banks and IT dominate early season
- **Success Rate**: 65% directional accuracy for Q4 trades

#### **Trading Considerations**
- **Position Sizing**: Reduce by 20-30% due to volatility
- **Sector Focus**: Financials and IT (60% of market cap)
- **Risk Management**: Wider stops (3-4% vs normal 2%)
- **Timing**: Avoid last 3 days of March (deadline pressure)

### Q1 Earnings Season (April-June)

#### **Season Characteristics**
- **Duration**: April 1 - June 30
- **Peak Period**: April (Q4 results) + June (Q1 results)
- **Market Volatility**: Moderate (20-30% increase)
- **Economic Context**: Monsoon outlook, currency effects

#### **Key Patterns**
- **Dual Peak**: April (annual) and June (quarterly)
- **IT Focus**: 40% of earnings reports in April-June
- **Revenue Sensitivity**: USD/INR impact on IT earnings
- **Success Rate**: 58% directional accuracy

#### **Trading Considerations**
- **Currency Hedge**: Consider INR positions for IT exposure
- **Estimate Quality**: Higher dispersion in Q1 estimates
- **Sector Balance**: Mix of IT, pharma, and industrials
- **Timing**: June earnings more predictable than April

### Q2 Earnings Season (July-September)

#### **Season Characteristics**
- **Duration**: July 1 - September 30
- **Peak Period**: July-August (festival season impact)
- **Market Volatility**: Low-moderate (10-20% increase)
- **Economic Context**: Festival spending, rural demand

#### **Key Patterns**
- **Consumer Focus**: FMCG and retail earnings prominent
- **Weather Impact**: Monsoon-dependent sectors volatile
- **Low Volume**: Summer holiday effect on trading
- **Success Rate**: 62% directional accuracy

#### **Trading Considerations**
- **Rural Economy**: Monitor monsoon progress
- **Consumer Discretionary**: Higher beta during festivals
- **Position Sizing**: Normal sizing (volatility lower)
- **Timing**: September often strongest month

### Q3 Earnings Season (October-December)

#### **Season Characteristics**
- **Duration**: October 1 - December 31
- **Peak Period**: October (Diwali effect) + December year-end
- **Market Volatility**: Moderate-high (25-35% increase)
- **Economic Context**: Year-end tax planning, budget expectations

#### **Key Patterns**
- **Festive Season**: Diwali impact on consumer earnings
- **Year-End Focus**: Tax planning affects Q3 results
- **Advance Tax**: Companies accelerate expenses
- **Success Rate**: 60% directional accuracy

#### **Trading Considerations**
- **Tax Effects**: Q3 often shows inflated expenses
- **Festival Impact**: Consumer sectors 20-30% higher
- **Options Activity**: Higher open interest in December
- **Timing**: October strongest for momentum plays

## Sector-Specific Earnings Patterns

- **Q2 (Sep)**: 25% (June quarter - monsoon effect)
- **Q3 (Dec)**: 20% (September quarter)
- **Q4 (Mar)**: 20% (December quarter - year-end)

#### **Key Drivers**

- **USD/INR Impact**: 2-3% earnings change per rupee

- **Retail Interest**: Social media sentiment spikes

#### **Volatility Dynamics**
- **IV Expansion**: 5-10 points increase typical
- **Skew Changes**: Negative skew develops for bearish bias
- **Term Structure**: Front month options most active
- **Correlation**: Sector correlation increases

#### **Price Action**
- **Range Expansion**: 2-3% wider daily ranges
- **Support/Resistance**: Key levels tested
- **Gap Risk**: 40% probability of earnings gap
- **Momentum**: Pre-earnings drift visible

## Seasonal Earnings Anomalies

### Calendar Month Effects

#### **January Effect**
- **Pattern**: Small cap earnings outperform large cap
- **Reason**: Tax-loss harvesting, year-end positioning
- **Performance**: Small caps +15% vs large caps +5%
- **Duration**: First 15 trading days

#### **March Madness**
- **Pattern**: Extreme volatility in last week
- **Reason**: Results deadline, tax planning
- **Performance**: 70% of annual earnings move
- **Duration**: Last 5 trading days

#### **April Blues**
- **Pattern**: IT sector underperformance
- **Reason**: Revenue recognition, currency effects
- **Performance**: IT index -5% vs market +2%
- **Duration**: First 10 trading days

#### **September Surprise**
- **Pattern**: Positive earnings momentum
- **Reason**: Festival season, good monsoon
- **Performance**: Consumer sectors +8% outperformance
- **Duration**: September earnings season

### Holiday Effects

#### **Diwali Impact**
- **Timing**: October-November earnings
- **Effect**: Consumer sector +20-30% earnings boost
- **Duration**: 2-week window around Diwali
- **Sectors**: FMCG, retail, consumer discretionary

#### **Budget Impact**
- **Timing**: Post-Union Budget (February)
- **Effect**: Market-sensitive sectors volatile
- **Duration**: 1-week post-budget
- **Sectors**: PSU banks, infrastructure, capital goods

#### **Monsoon Effect**
- **Timing**: Q1 earnings (June quarter)
- **Effect**: Rural-dependent sectors volatile
- **Duration**: June-August monsoon season
- **Sectors**: FMCG, agriculture, rural banking

## Risk Management During Earnings Season

### Portfolio Construction

#### **Diversification Rules**
- **Sector Limit**: Maximum 25% in single sector
- **Company Limit**: Maximum 5% in single company
- **Earnings Exposure**: Maximum 30% in earnings plays
- **Liquidity Requirement**: Minimum 60% in liquid stocks

#### **Position Sizing Adjustments**
- **Q4 Season**: 70% of normal position size
- **High Volatility**: 50% reduction for VIX > 25
- **Low Liquidity**: 30% reduction for stocks < ₹500cr market cap
- **Options Exposure**: 20% of normal equity exposure

### Stop Loss Strategies

#### **Pre-Earnings Stops**
- **Technical Levels**: 2.5% below key support
- **Time-Based**: Exit if earnings delayed >5 days
- **Volatility Stops**: 2× ATR distance
- **Portfolio Stops**: 6% maximum drawdown

#### **Post-Earnings Stops**
- **Trailing Stops**: 4% trailing for momentum plays
- **Time Stops**: Exit after 5 days if no follow-through
- **Gap Protection**: Wider stops for after-hours earnings
- **Sector Stops**: Exit sector if 3+ companies miss

### Volatility Management

#### **VIX-Based Adjustments**
- **VIX < 20**: Normal position sizing
- **VIX 20-30**: 80% position sizing
- **VIX 30-40**: 60% position sizing
- **VIX > 40**: 40% position sizing or avoid

#### **Implied Move Adjustments**
- **IM < 3%**: Normal sizing
- **IM 3-5%**: 90% sizing
- **IM 5-8%**: 75% sizing
- **IM > 8%**: 50% sizing or options only
