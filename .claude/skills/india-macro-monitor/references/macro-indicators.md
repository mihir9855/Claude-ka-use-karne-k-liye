# Indian Macro Indicators - Complete Reference Guide

## Primary Macro Indicators

### CPI Inflation (Consumer Price Index)

**Definition**: Measures changes in retail prices of goods and services consumed by households
**Frequency**: Monthly, released mid-month
**Source**: National Statistical Office (NSO)
**Base Year**: 2012 = 100
**Target**: RBI target 4% ± 2% (2-6% range)

**Components & Weights**:
- Food & Beverages: 45.86%
- Fuel & Light: 6.84%
- Housing: 10.07%
- Clothing & Footwear: 6.53%
- Health: 5.89%
- Transport & Communication: 7.91%
- Recreation & Education: 5.92%
- Personal Care: 4.48%
- Miscellaneous: 6.50%

**Market Impact**:
- **>6%**: Hawkish RBI, rate hike expectations, Nifty pressure
- **4-6%**: Data-dependent, watch RBI commentary
- **<4%**: Dovish bias, rate cut possible
- **Historical Reaction**: Nifty -0.8% average when CPI > expectations

**Key Insights**:
- Food inflation most volatile (monsoon dependent)
- Core inflation (excluding food/fuel) more persistent
- Rural inflation often leads urban
- Fuel inflation directly linked to crude oil prices

### WPI Inflation (Wholesale Price Index)

**Definition**: Measures changes in wholesale prices of goods traded in bulk
**Frequency**: Monthly, released mid-month
**Source**: Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI)
**Base Year**: 2011-12 = 100

**Components & Weights**:
- Primary Articles: 22.62% (food, non-food agriculture)
- Fuel & Power: 13.15% (coal, electricity, petroleum)
- Manufactured Products: 64.23% (manufacturing goods)

**Market Impact**:
- **Leading Indicator**: Precedes CPI by 1-2 months
- **Fuel Component**: Direct crude oil impact
- **Manufacturing**: Economic activity signal
- **Historical Correlation**: 0.7 correlation with CPI

**Key Insights**:
- Fuel inflation most volatile component
- Primary articles reflect agricultural costs
- Manufactured products indicate industrial health

### GDP Growth

**Definition**: Gross Domestic Product measures total economic output
**Frequency**: Quarterly (provisional), annual revision
**Source**: National Statistical Office (NSO)
**Base Year**: 2011-12

**Components**:
- Agriculture: 15-20% of GDP
- Industry: 25-30% (manufacturing, construction, mining)
- Services: 50-55% (IT, finance, real estate, government)

**Market Impact**:
- **>6.5%**: Bullish for cyclicals (IT, metals, auto)
- **4-6.5%**: Mixed environment, defensives preferred
- **<4%**: Bearish, pharma/IT safe havens
- **Historical Reaction**: Nifty +1.5% on GDP beats

**Key Insights**:
- Services most stable component
- Agriculture monsoon dependent
- Industry sensitive to global demand

### RBI Repo Rate

**Definition**: Rate at which RBI lends to banks for short-term
**Frequency**: Bi-monthly (6 times per year)
**Current Level**: 6.50% (as of Dec 2023)
**Impact**: Influences all borrowing costs in economy

**Transmission Mechanism**:
- **Banks**: Adjust lending rates (home loans, auto loans)
- **Deposits**: Impact savings rates
- **Corporate**: Cost of borrowing for companies
- **Government**: G-sec yields and fiscal costs

**Market Impact Matrix**:
```
Rate Cut (25bps):
- Banks: +3-5% (NIM expansion)
- NBFCs: +2-4% (cheaper funding)
- Auto: +2-3% (higher financing)
- Realty: +2-3% (lower home loans)

Rate Hike (25bps):
- Banks: -2-4% (NIM compression)
- Auto: -2-3% (higher EMIs)
- Realty: -3-5% (higher home loans)
```

### GST Collections

**Definition**: Goods and Services Tax revenue collected monthly
**Frequency**: Monthly, released mid-next month
**Source**: Central Board of Indirect Taxes (CBIC)
**Trend Level**: ₹1.4-1.6 lakh crores per month

**Components**:
- CGST: Central GST
- SGST: State GST
- IGST: Inter-state GST
- Cess: Additional levies

**Market Impact**:
- **Above Trend**: Economic strength signal, Nifty +0.5-1%
- **Below Trend**: Growth concerns, defensive sectors
- **Historical Average**: ₹1.45L crores (2022-23)

**Key Insights**:
- Festival months show higher collections
- E-commerce growth visible in IGST
- Industrial production correlation: 0.6

### Forex Reserves

**Definition**: Foreign currency assets held by RBI
**Frequency**: Weekly (every Friday)
**Current Level**: ~$578 billion (Dec 2023)
**Components**: Foreign currency, gold, SDRs, IMF position

**Market Impact**:
- **Rising**: Currency stability, bullish for importers
- **Falling**: INR pressure, IT/pharma benefit
- **Adequate Level**: 10-12 months of imports

**Key Insights**:
- FII flows major driver
- Crude oil imports biggest use
- Gold reserves provide safety buffer

### Crude Oil (Brent)

**Definition**: Global benchmark for crude oil prices
**Frequency**: Daily trading
**Current Level**: ~$80-85 per barrel
**India Impact**: 85% of consumption imported

**Price Impact on India**:
```
$10 increase in Brent:
- Current account deficit: +$10-12B widening
- Inflation: +0.25-0.35% (CPI impact)
- Energy sector: +2-3% stock impact
- Auto/FMCG: -1-2% (input cost pressure)
```

**Market Impact**:
- **Rising Oil**: Energy stocks rally, broader market pressure
- **Falling Oil**: Consumption sectors benefit, current account improves
- **Volatility**: Geopolitical events drive spikes

## Secondary Macro Indicators

### IIP (Index of Industrial Production)

**Definition**: Measures industrial production across manufacturing, mining, electricity
**Frequency**: Monthly, released mid-next month
**Source**: Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation
**Base Year**: 2011-12 = 100

**Components**:
- Manufacturing: 77.6% weight
- Mining: 14.4% weight
- Electricity: 8.0% weight

**Market Impact**:
- **Above Trend**: Manufacturing health, cyclicals benefit
- **Below Trend**: Economic slowdown concerns
- **Correlation with GDP**: 0.7 (industrial component)

### Core Inflation

**Definition**: CPI inflation excluding food and fuel components
**Frequency**: Monthly (same as CPI)
**Source**: RBI calculations
**Significance**: More persistent inflation measure

**Market Impact**:
- **Rising Core**: Structural inflation concerns
- **Stable Core**: RBI comfort with rate cuts
- **RBI Focus**: Core inflation in policy decisions

### Current Account Deficit

**Definition**: Difference between exports and imports of goods/services
**Frequency**: Quarterly
**Source**: RBI
**Sustainable Level**: 2-3% of GDP

**Components**:
- Merchandise trade deficit
- Services surplus (IT, software)
- Remittances, investments

**Market Impact**:
- **Widening CAD**: INR depreciation pressure
- **Narrowing CAD**: Currency stability
- **Financing**: Through FII flows and remittances

### Fiscal Deficit

**Definition**: Government borrowing requirement
**Frequency**: Monthly (budgeted annually)
**Source**: Ministry of Finance
**Target**: 5.9% of GDP (FY24)

**Components**:
- Revenue deficit
- Capital expenditure
- Interest payments

**Market Impact**:
- **Above Target**: Bond yield pressure, crowding out
- **Below Target**: Fiscal prudence, lower yields
- **Bond Market**: Direct impact on G-sec yields

### 10-Year G-Sec Yield

**Definition**: Yield on 10-year Indian government bonds
**Frequency**: Daily trading
**Current Level**: 7.2-7.4%
**Significance**: Proxy for interest rate expectations

**Market Impact**:
- **Rising Yields**: Hawkish expectations, banking stocks pressure
- **Falling Yields**: Dovish expectations, financials rally
- **Global Correlation**: US 10Y yield movements

## Market Reaction Patterns

### Event-Based Reactions

#### **RBI Policy Day**
- **Pre-meeting**: Nifty tends to rise 2 weeks before
- **Announcement**: Volatile first 2 hours, direction by 1 PM
- **Post-meeting**: Trend persists 2-4 weeks
- **Success Rate**: 60% directional accuracy

#### **Inflation Data**
- **Beat Expectations**: 40% negative reaction probability
- **Miss Expectations**: 55% positive reaction probability
- **Impact Duration**: 2-3 trading sessions

#### **GDP Data**
- **Q4 Impact**: Highest (budget, earnings season)
- **Sector Rotation**: Cyclicals on beats, defensives on misses
- **Duration**: 1-2 weeks for full impact

### Seasonal Patterns

#### **Monsoon Impact**
- **June-September**: Rural inflation, FMCG performance
- **GDP Impact**: Agriculture component volatility
- **Market Effect**: FMCG stocks 2-3% premium in good monsoon

#### **Festival Season**
- **October-November**: GST collections peak
- **Consumption**: Auto, FMCG, retail benefit
- **Market Effect**: Broad market rally potential

#### **Budget Season**
- **January-February**: Pre-budget rally
- **Post-budget**: Sector-specific rotation
- **Duration**: 4-6 weeks impact

- **Leading Indicators**: WPI, forex reserves, crude oil

- **Lagging**: GDP, current account

- **Alerts**: Automated notifications for critical breaches
- **Scenarios**: Stress test portfolios against macro shocks

### Integration
- **Technical Analysis**: Combine with support/resistance levels
- **Fundamental Analysis**: Link to sector earnings expectations
- **Options Strategies**: Volatility plays around high-impact events
