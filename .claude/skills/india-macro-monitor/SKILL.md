# India Macro Monitor - Economic Environment Tracker

## Overview

The India Macro Monitor tracks key economic indicators and central bank policy to provide comprehensive insights into the Indian economic environment. This skill analyzes how macroeconomic factors influence market direction and sector performance.

## Core Capabilities

### RBI Policy Tracking
- **MPC Meeting Calendar**: Next meeting dates and expected decisions
- **Policy Stance Analysis**: Current rate stance (hawkish/neutral/dovish)
- **Rate Decision Impact**: Historical market reactions to rate changes
- **Policy Transmission**: How rate decisions affect different sectors

### Inflation Monitoring
- **CPI Analysis**: Consumer Price Index trends and forecasts
- **WPI Tracking**: Wholesale Price Index as leading indicator
- **Inflation Expectations**: Market and household inflation views
- **RBI Inflation Target**: 4% ± 2% compliance assessment

### Economic Indicators
- **GDP Growth**: Quarterly GDP data and growth projections
- **GST Collections**: Monthly government revenue tracking
- **Forex Reserves**: Foreign exchange reserves and currency trends
- **Current Account**: Trade balance and external sector health

### Commodity & Currency Impact
- **Crude Oil Monitoring**: Brent crude prices and INR sensitivity
- **INR/USD Analysis**: Currency fluctuations and market impact
- **Import Bill Tracking**: Energy and gold import cost analysis

## Analysis Framework

### RBI Policy Framework

#### **MPC Composition**
- **6 Members**: 3 RBI officials + 3 external experts
- **Meeting Frequency**: Every 2 months (6 meetings per year)
- **Decision Types**: Rate unchanged, 25bps cut/hike, pause with stance change

#### **Policy Stance Interpretation**
- **Withdrawal**: Actively fighting inflation (hawkish)
- **Neutral**: Balanced approach, data-dependent
- **Accommodative**: Supporting growth (dovish)

#### **Rate Decision Impact**
```
Dovish Outcome (Rate Cut/Hold):
- Nifty +60% probability of positive reaction
- Banks rally 3-5% (NIM expansion)
- Auto/Realty benefit from lower borrowing costs
- Duration: 2-4 weeks for full impact

Hawkish Outcome (Rate Hike):
- Nifty -40% probability of negative reaction
- Financials under pressure
- Defensive sectors (FMCG/Pharma) relatively better
```

### Inflation Analysis

#### **CPI Components & Impact**
- **Food**: 45% weight, monsoon dependent, volatile
- **Fuel**: 7% weight, directly linked to crude oil
- **Core**: 48% weight, services inflation, persistent

#### **RBI Reaction Function**
```
CPI > 6%: Hawkish bias, rate hike likely
CPI 4-6%: Data-dependent, watch WPI trends
CPI < 4%: Dovish bias, rate cut possible
```

### Economic Growth Tracking

#### **GDP Components**
- **Agriculture**: 15-20% weight, monsoon dependent
- **Industry**: 25-30% weight, manufacturing + construction
- **Services**: 50-55% weight, most stable component

#### **Growth Cycle Impact**
```
>6.5% GDP: Bullish for cyclicals (IT, metals, auto)
4-6.5% GDP: Mixed, defensive sectors preferred
<4% GDP: Bearish, pharma/IT safe havens
```

## Implementation Details

### Data Sources
- **RBI Website**: Policy statements, inflation data, forex reserves
- **NSO/MoSPI**: CPI, WPI, GDP, IIP data
- **CBIC**: GST collection data
- **Global Sources**: Brent crude prices, USD/INR rates

### Analysis Frequency
- **Daily**: Crude oil, currency, bond yields
- **Weekly**: Forex reserves, market inflation expectations
- **Monthly**: CPI, WPI, GST collections, IIP
- **Quarterly**: GDP, current account data
- **Bi-monthly**: RBI policy decisions

### Risk Assessment
- **Inflation Risk**: CPI >6% triggers hawkish concerns
- **Growth Risk**: GDP <5% indicates economic slowdown
- **Currency Risk**: INR depreciation >2% in month
- **Fiscal Risk**: GST collections below trend
