# VCP Quality Scoring System

## Overview
The VCP scoring system evaluates pattern quality on a 0-100 point scale across five key dimensions. Higher scores indicate stronger accumulation patterns with higher breakout probability and reward potential.

## Scoring Components (20 points each)

### 1. Trend Template (0-20 points)
Evaluates adherence to Minervini Trend Template criteria.

**Scoring Breakdown:**
- **Price > 150 DMA**: 4 points
- **Price > 200 DMA**: 4 points
- **200 DMA Rising**: 4 points
- **Within 25% of 52-week high**: 4 points
- **30%+ above 52-week low**: 2 points
- **RS Rating >70**: 2 points

**Perfect Score**: 20 points (all criteria met)
**Minimum Acceptable**: 16 points (missing 1 minor criterion)

### 2. Contraction Quality (0-20 points)
Assesses the technical quality of price contractions.

**Scoring Breakdown:**
- **Number of Contractions**:
  - 4+ contractions: 8 points
  - 3 contractions: 6 points
  - 2 contractions: 4 points
- **Depth Reduction**:
  - >60% shallower each time: 6 points
  - 40-60% shallower: 4 points
  - 30-40% shallower: 2 points
- **Pattern Shape**:
  - Ascending triangle/wedge: 4 points
  - Irregular but valid: 2 points
- **Duration**:
  - 6-10 weeks: 2 points (optimal)
  - 3-6 weeks or 10-12 weeks: 1 point

**Perfect Score**: 20 points (4+ contractions, >60% reduction, ideal shape/duration)

### 3. Volume Profile (0-20 points)
Analyzes volume patterns throughout the base formation.

**Scoring Breakdown:**
- **Base Volume Trend**:
  - Declining through base: 6 points
  - Mixed but ending lower: 4 points
  - Increasing: 0 points
- **Final Contraction Volume**:
  - <40% of 50-day avg: 6 points
  - 40-50% of 50-day avg: 4 points
  - 50-60% of 50-day avg: 2 points
- **Volume Spikes**:
  - No distribution spikes: 4 points
  - Minor spikes: 2 points
  - Major distribution: 0 points
- **Breakout Volume Potential**:
  - Above average volume history: 4 points
  - Average volume: 2 points

**Perfect Score**: 20 points (declining base, extreme dry-up, no distribution)

### 4. Fundamental Score (0-20 points)
Validates company growth and financial health.

**Scoring Breakdown:**
- **EPS Growth**:
  - 3+ quarters positive QoQ: 6 points
  - 2 quarters positive QoQ: 4 points
  - 1 quarter positive: 2 points
- **Revenue Growth**:
  - YoY growth >15%: 4 points
  - YoY growth 5-15%: 3 points
  - YoY growth 0-5%: 1 point
- **Margin Trend**:
  - Expanding margins: 4 points
  - Stable margins: 3 points
  - Declining margins: 0 points
- **Business Quality**:
  - Strong competitive position: 3 points
  - Average business: 2 points
  - Weak fundamentals: 0 points

**Perfect Score**: 20 points (strong EPS growth, revenue growth, margin expansion)

### 5. Tightness (0-20 points)
Measures the compactness of the final contraction.

**Scoring Breakdown:**
- **Final Contraction Range**:
  - <5% of breakout price: 10 points
  - 5-7% of breakout price: 8 points
  - 7-10% of breakout price: 6 points
  - 10-15% of breakout price: 4 points
- **Price Action Quality**:
  - Very tight, sideways: 6 points
  - Minor volatility: 4 points
  - Choppy action: 2 points
- **Time Factor**:
  - 2-3 weeks duration: 4 points
  - 1-2 weeks or 3-4 weeks: 2 points

**Perfect Score**: 20 points (<5% range, very tight action, optimal duration)

## Grade Classification

### A+ Grade (90-100 points)
- **Characteristics**: Perfect or near-perfect in all categories
- **Position Size**: 100% of normal allocation
- **Conviction Level**: Maximum - these are "once-in-a-career" setups
- **Expected Performance**: 80-90% breakout success rate
- **Risk Management**: Standard 5-8% stop loss

### A Grade (80-89 points)
- **Characteristics**: Excellent in most categories, minor weakness in one
- **Position Size**: 75-80% of normal allocation
- **Conviction Level**: Very high - strong accumulation patterns
- **Expected Performance**: 70-80% breakout success rate
- **Risk Management**: Standard 5-8% stop loss

### A- Grade (70-79 points)
- **Characteristics**: Good overall, acceptable weaknesses
- **Position Size**: 60-70% of normal allocation
- **Conviction Level**: High - solid setups with minor concerns
- **Expected Performance**: 65-75% breakout success rate
- **Risk Management**: 6-8% stop loss (slightly wider)

### B+ Grade (60-69 points)
- **Characteristics**: Above average, some notable weaknesses
- **Position Size**: 40-50% of normal allocation
- **Conviction Level**: Moderate - acceptable but not ideal
- **Expected Performance**: 55-65% breakout success rate
- **Risk Management**: 7-9% stop loss

### B Grade (50-59 points)
- **Characteristics**: Average quality, multiple weaknesses
- **Position Size**: 25-35% of normal allocation
- **Conviction Level**: Low-moderate - marginal setups
- **Expected Performance**: 45-55% breakout success rate
- **Risk Management**: 8-10% stop loss

### C Grade (Below 50 points)
- **Characteristics**: Poor quality, significant flaws
- **Position Size**: Skip - do not trade
- **Conviction Level**: None - avoid these setups
- **Expected Performance**: <40% breakout success rate
- **Risk Management**: Not applicable

## Market Regime Adjustments

### Bull Market Scoring
- **Higher Standards**: Require 85+ for A+ grade
- **Volume Weight**: Increase volume component weighting
- **Fundamental Weight**: Slightly reduce fundamental requirements
- **Success Rate**: Higher breakout probability in bull markets

### Neutral Market Scoring
- **Standard Application**: Use base scoring system
- **Reduced Size**: All grades get 20-30% smaller positions
- **Tighter Stops**: Implement 6-8% stops across all grades
- **Success Rate**: Moderate breakout probability

### Bear Market Scoring
- **Stricter Standards**: Require 95+ for A+ grade (rare)
- **Defensive Bias**: Reduce position sizes by 50%
- **Wide Stops**: 10-12% stops due to higher volatility
- **Success Rate**: Low breakout probability - avoid most setups

## Dynamic Scoring Adjustments

### Time-Based Factors
- **Pattern Age**: Reduce score 2-3 points per week over 8 weeks
- **Market Position**: Adjust based on current market vs 52-week high
- **Sector Strength**: Boost score for strong sector leadership

### Technical Adjustments
- **Volume Context**: Adjust volume score based on overall market volume
- **Volatility Environment**: Modify tightness requirements in high volatility
- **Liquidity Factors**: Penalize low liquidity stocks

### Fundamental Updates
- **Recent Results**: Update scores after earnings releases
- **Guidance Changes**: Adjust for management commentary
- **Industry Trends**: Factor in sector-specific developments

## Score Interpretation Guidelines

### Score Ranges and Expectations
- **90-100**: Exceptional setups, allocate maximum capital
- **80-89**: Very strong setups, high conviction trades
- **70-79**: Good setups, normal allocation with caution
- **60-69**: Acceptable setups, reduced allocation
- **50-59**: Marginal setups, very small allocation
- **<50**: Poor setups, avoid completely

### Risk-Adjusted Position Sizing
```
Position Size = Base Size × (Score/100)² × Market Adjustment
```

Where:
- **Base Size**: 1-2% of portfolio for A+ setups
- **Score Factor**: Quadratic relationship (higher scores get disproportionately larger)
- **Market Adjustment**: 0.5-1.5 based on market regime

### Stop Loss by Score
- **90-100**: 5% below final contraction low
- **80-89**: 6% below final contraction low
- **70-79**: 7% below final contraction low
- **60-69**: 8% below final contraction low
- **50-59**: 9% below final contraction low

## Common Scoring Mistakes

### Over-Scoring Issues
- **Ignoring Volume**: Giving high scores despite poor volume profile
- **Fundamental Blindness**: Overlooking deteriorating financials
- **Pattern Perfection**: Penalizing good setups for minor imperfections

### Under-Scoring Issues
- **Overly Strict**: Rejecting good setups due to rigid criteria
- **Volume Confusion**: Penalizing normal volume fluctuations
- **Market Context**: Not adjusting for current market conditions

### Consistency Problems
- **Subjective Judgment**: Inconsistent application of criteria
- **Time Pressure**: Rushed scoring leading to errors
- **Fatigue Impact**: Lower quality scoring when tired
