# Indian Market Behavior — NotSoSEBI

## Budget Day Patterns
**High IV Crush Post 12:30 PM:** Volatility spikes pre-budget (10-11 AM), then rapid decay after announcement. Technical levels break easily due to gamma effects. Trade with extreme caution, avoid options.

## RBI Policy Day
**Volatility Spike at 10 AM:** Repo rate decisions cause immediate volatility bursts. Trend often continues in announcement direction for 1-2 hours. Monitor PCR for directional bias.

## Expiry Day Dynamics
**Theta Decay Acceleration:** Options lose value rapidly after 2 PM. Max pain convergence visible in last hour. Intraday technicals less reliable — focus on weekly charts.

## Election Result Day
**Gap + Trend Day:** Massive gaps on result morning, then strong directional moves. Volume 2-3x normal. Technical analysis suspended — pure momentum trading.

## FII Bulk Dealing Days
**Momentum Follows for 2-3 Sessions:** Large FII buys/sells create sustained trends. NIFTY often moves 1-2% in follow-through. Watch delivery volumes for sustainability.

## Monday Gap Patterns
**Weekend News Gap:** Significant gaps due to global events. Gap fill probability 70% by Wednesday. Monday afternoon often sees reversal attempts.

## Friday Profit Booking
**Tendency to Sell:** Profit-taking visible after 2 PM. Technical supports often break. Avoid long positions heading into weekend.

## Pre-Holiday Risk-Off
**Conservative Behavior:** Reduced volumes, risk-aversion. Last trading day before holiday shows profit-booking. Gaps often fill on holiday resumption.
