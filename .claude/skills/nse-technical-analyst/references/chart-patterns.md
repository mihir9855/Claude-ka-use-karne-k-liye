# Chart Patterns Guide — NotSoSEBI

## Head & Shoulders (H&S)
**Identification:** Left shoulder, head (higher high), right shoulder (similar to left), neckline connects lows
**Volume Confirmation:** Higher on left shoulder, lower on right shoulder
**Target Calculation:** Height of pattern projected below neckline
**Failure Rate:** 65% (common in NSE due to FII selling)

## Inverse Head & Shoulders
**Identification:** Mirror of H&S - left shoulder, head (lower low), right shoulder, neckline connects highs
**Volume Confirmation:** Higher volume on right shoulder breakout
**Target Calculation:** Height of pattern projected above neckline
**Failure Rate:** 60% (works better in downtrends)

## Double Top
**Identification:** Two peaks at similar level, valley between them
**Volume Confirmation:** Decreasing volume on second peak
**Target Calculation:** Height from valley to peak, projected below valley
**Failure Rate:** 70% (NSE often fails due to momentum)

## Double Bottom
**Identification:** Two troughs at similar level, peak between them
**Volume Confirmation:** Increasing volume on second trough
**Target Calculation:** Height from peak to trough, projected above peak
**Failure Rate:** 65% (more reliable than double top)

## Flags
**Identification:** Sharp move followed by parallel consolidation (5-15 bars)
**Volume Confirmation:** Low volume during consolidation
**Target Calculation:** Flagpole height projected from breakout point
**Failure Rate:** 55% (continuation pattern, reliable in trends)

## Pennants
**Identification:** Sharp move into converging triangle consolidation
**Volume Confirmation:** Very low volume during pennant formation
**Target Calculation:** Pennant height projected from breakout
**Failure Rate:** 50% (most reliable continuation pattern)

## Ascending Triangle
**Identification:** Horizontal resistance, rising support, volume increases on rallies
**Volume Confirmation:** Higher volume near apex
**Target Calculation:** Height at widest part projected from breakout
**Failure Rate:** 60% (bullish bias, works in uptrends)

## Descending Triangle
**Identification:** Horizontal support, declining resistance
**Volume Confirmation:** Higher volume on declines
**Target Calculation:** Height projected from breakdown point
**Failure Rate:** 65% (bearish bias, less reliable)

## Wedges
**Identification:** Converging trendlines (rising wedge = bearish, falling wedge = bullish)
**Volume Confirmation:** Decreasing volume in wedge, spike on breakout
**Target Calculation:** Wedge height projected from apex
**Failure Rate:** 55% (direction depends on slope)

## Cup and Handle
**Identification:** U-shaped cup followed by small consolidation handle
**Volume Confirmation:** High volume during right side of cup
**Target Calculation:** Cup depth × 2-3 projected from breakout
**Failure Rate:** 45% (very reliable in bull markets)

## Rounding Bottom
**Identification:** Smooth U-shaped curve from resistance to support
**Volume Confirmation:** Low volume in middle, increasing toward end
**Target Calculation:** Pattern height projected from breakout
**Failure Rate:** 50% (works well in individual stocks)</content>
