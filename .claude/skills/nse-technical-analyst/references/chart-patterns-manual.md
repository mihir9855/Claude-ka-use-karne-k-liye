# Chart Patterns Manual for NSE Trading

## Overview
Comprehensive guide to chart patterns observed in Indian equity markets. Includes recognition, measurement techniques, and success probabilities specific to NSE stocks and indices.

## Reversal Patterns

### Head and Shoulders
**Structure:**
- Left shoulder: Price peak, followed by decline
- Head: Higher peak than shoulders
- Right shoulder: Similar height to left shoulder
- Neckline: Trendline connecting lows between peaks

**Measurement:**
- Target = Neckline - (Head - Neckline)
- Volume: Should increase on right shoulder breakout

**NSE Context:**
- Reliable in NIFTY 50 stocks
- False breakouts common - wait for volume confirmation
- Success rate: 65-75% when properly formed

**Example:** RELIANCE, TCS often show clear H&S patterns

### Double Top/Bottom
**Structure:**
- Two peaks (top) or troughs (bottom) at similar price levels
- Volume: Decreasing on second peak/trough
- Break below/above neckline confirms pattern

**Measurement:**
- Target = Entry point - (Height × 0.618 Fibonacci)
- Stop loss: Above/below the second peak/trough

**NSE Context:**
- Common in range-bound markets
- BANKNIFTY shows frequent double bottoms
- Success rate: 70% with volume confirmation

### Triple Top/Bottom
**Structure:**
- Three peaks/troughs at same level
- More reliable than double patterns
- Volume should diminish on each peak

**NSE Context:**
- Less common but more reliable when formed
- Often seen in large-cap stocks during distribution phases

## Continuation Patterns

### Flags and Pennants
**Bull Flag:**
- Sharp upward move (flagpole)
- Consolidation in downward sloping channel
- Breakout to upside with volume

**Bear Flag:**
- Sharp downward move
- Consolidation in upward sloping channel
- Breakdown with volume

**Measurement:**
- Target = Flagpole height projected from breakout point
- Duration: 1-3 weeks typically

**NSE Context:**
- Common in momentum stocks like ADANIPORTS
- Success rate: 60-70%
- False breakouts frequent - use tight stops

### Wedges
**Rising Wedge (Bearish):**
- Higher highs, higher lows but slope converging
- Breakout usually downward
- Volume: Decreasing during formation

**Falling Wedge (Bullish):**
- Lower highs, lower lows but slope converging
- Breakout usually upward
- Volume: Should increase on breakout

**NSE Context:**
- Reliable in trending markets
- NIFTY often forms wedges during corrections

### Triangles
**Symmetrical Triangle:**
- Converging trendlines with no clear bias
- Breakout in direction of prior trend
- Volume: Low during formation, high on breakout

**Ascending Triangle (Bullish):**
- Horizontal resistance, rising support
- Breakout usually upward
- Volume confirmation crucial

**Descending Triangle (Bearish):**
- Horizontal support, falling resistance
- Breakout usually downward

**NSE Context:**
- Symmetrical triangles common in NIFTY
- Ascending triangles work well in bull markets
- Success rate: 65-75%

## Candlestick Patterns

### Single Candlestick Patterns

**Marubozu (Bullish/Bearish):**
- Long body, no shadows
- Indicates strong directional move
- Confirmation needed with volume

**Doji:**
- Open = Close, long shadows
- Indecision, potential reversal
- More significant at support/resistance

**Hammer/Shooting Star:**
- Hammer: Long lower shadow, small body (bullish at bottom)
- Shooting Star: Long upper shadow, small body (bearish at top)

**NSE Context:**
- Doji at key levels very reliable
- Marubozu in NIFTY indicates institutional activity

### Multiple Candlestick Patterns

**Engulfing Pattern:**
- Bullish: Small red candle engulfed by large green
- Bearish: Small green candle engulfed by large red
- Volume: Higher on engulfing candle

**Morning/Evening Star:**
- Three candles: Large body, small body (doji/spinning top), opposite large body
- Morning star: Bottom reversal
- Evening star: Top reversal

**Piercing Cloud/Dark Cloud Cover:**
- Piercing: Green candle pierces midpoint of previous red
- Dark Cloud: Red candle covers midpoint of previous green

**NSE Context:**
- Engulfing patterns work well in volatile stocks
- Star patterns reliable in NIFTY during trends

## Price Action Patterns

### Inside Bar
**Structure:**
- Current bar completely within previous bar's range
- Indicates consolidation before breakout
- Breakout direction gives signal

**NSE Context:**
- Very reliable in Indian markets
- Often precedes earnings moves
- Success rate: 70-80%

### Pin Bar
**Structure:**
- Long shadow (nose) opposite to direction
- Small body near one end
- Indicates rejection of price level

**Bullish Pin:** Long upper shadow, body near low
**Bearish Pin:** Long lower shadow, body near high

**NSE Context:**
- Excellent for identifying key levels
- Works well with support/resistance

### Fakey Pattern
**Structure:**
- Inside bar followed by breakout
- But breakout fails and reverses
- Strong reversal signal

**NSE Context:**
- High success rate but requires patience
- Common in ranging markets

## NSE Market Specific Patterns

### Gap Patterns
**Common Gap:** Filled within few days
**Breakaway Gap:** Occurs at end of consolidation, not filled easily
**Exhaustion Gap:** Against trend, signals reversal
**Island Reversal:** Gap up then gap down (or vice versa)

**NSE Context:**
- Gaps often occur after earnings or news
- Weekend gaps are significant
- Success rate: 60-70% for breakaway gaps

### NR7 Pattern (Narrow Range 7)
**Structure:**
- Current bar has smallest range in last 7 bars
- Indicates low volatility, potential breakout
- More significant if volume is low

**NSE Context:**
- Works well in NIFTY and large caps
- Often precedes directional moves
- Success rate: 65%

### Volume Price Analysis (VPA)
**High Volume at High Price:** Accumulation
**High Volume at Low Price:** Distribution
**Low Volume:** No institutional interest
**Ultra High Volume:** Climax, potential reversal

**NSE Context:**
- Critical for understanding institutional activity
- Volume spikes often indicate FII/DII moves

## Risk Management with Patterns

### Position Sizing
- Conservative: Risk 1% of capital per trade
- Aggressive: Risk 2% based on pattern strength
- Pattern-based: Larger position for high-probability patterns

### Stop Loss Placement
- Below neckline for reversals
- Above/below pattern for breakouts
- 1-2×ATR from entry for safety

### Target Setting
- Pattern measurement techniques
- Fibonacci extensions (1.272, 1.618)
- Previous resistance/support levels

## Common Mistakes

### Premature Entry
- Wait for confirmation (breakout + volume)
- Don't enter on first test of level

### Ignoring Context
- Same pattern behaves differently in bull vs bear markets
- Sector rotation affects pattern reliability

### Overtrading
- Not every pattern leads to trade
- Quality over quantity

### Wrong Timeframe
- Daily patterns on 5-min charts give too many signals
- 5-min patterns on daily charts are noise

## Seasonal Pattern Considerations

### Earnings Season (Jan, Apr, Jul, Oct)
- Patterns more reliable after results
- Volume analysis crucial
- Gaps common

### Budget Session (Feb-Mar)
- Increased volatility affects pattern formation
- False breakouts more common
- Use wider stops

### Festive Season (Oct-Nov)
- Lower volumes, choppy patterns
- Focus on large-cap stocks
- Avoid small patterns
