04Our story

The Studio

Where inherited craft meets precise design.

Our family has worked with stone for generations. Our great-grandfather and grandfather worked on the Delwara Deras at Mount Abu. It was there that our great-grandfather and his fellow craftsmen completed the murti of Shree Chakreshwari Devi, work that took them two years. That is where our journey with stone began, among some of the finest marble carving anywhere.

Later, our grandfather travelled all over India for work. He lived in Mount Abu, Surendranagar, Renukoot, Kutch, Mumbai, Shamlaji, Taranga Jain Derasar and many other places, carving murtis wherever the work took him. He worked on the temple the Birlas built at Ulhasnagar in Mumbai, and he carved murtis for the Somnath Temple too.

A temple is made through three kinds of work. Roopkaam is the carving of murtis and figures, Nakshikaam is the fine decorative carving, and Ghaatkaam is the shaping of the stone's mouldings and forms. Our grandfathers did Roopkaam, and today we do all three ourselves.

There is one rule we always keep: we never use metal anywhere in a temple. Every temple we build is made of stone alone.

Today Brihad Temple Construction Co. works on temple architecture, stone carving, sculpture and visualization. Before we draw anything, we try to understand what the place means to the people who will pray there. Then we work out the proportions, the stone and the details. We work all over India, in whichever stone your temple needs, and every piece is carved by hand by our own artisans. With the skill our elders handed down and the precision of today's tools, we build temples that will stand for generations.
