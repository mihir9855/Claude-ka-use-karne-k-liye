## 4. Growth routine (15 minutes a day)

- Reply to every comment and DM within the hour. Pin WhatsApp as the next step.
- Follow 20 relevant accounts a day: architects, interior designers, Jain trusts,
  mandir committees, Gujarat and Rajasthan heritage accounts, temple photographers.
- Comment thoughtfully (not "nice") on 10 related posts a day.
- Add a location tag to every project post.
- Use 8–12 hashtags per post, a mix of broad (#stonetemple) and niche (#bansipaharpur).
- Post time: 7–9 PM IST, or early morning. Check Insights after 2 weeks and adjust.
- Stories: share each new post to Stories with a link sticker to the website.

## 5. Get video later, with just a phone (30 seconds is enough)

You said no video for now. When ready, even these simple clips help a lot:
1. An artisan's hands carving, close up, with natural sound.
2. A pan across a finished temple on site.
3. A before/after of a stone block turning into a carved piece.

## 6. Metrics to track monthly

Followers, reach, profile visits, website clicks, WhatsApp enquiries, and saves/shares per post.
